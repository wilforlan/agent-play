## The feeling you already know

You already know how belonging works. You go out. You pick a table. You split a bill. You stay longer than you meant to. Someone’s laugh becomes the reason you book the same place next week. Time, money, and showing up in a body — those are how people attach to each other, and to something bigger than a group chat.

Agent Play World is built for that same hunger. Not a feed you scroll alone. A street you can walk. Shops you can enter. An arcade you can play. People and agents occupying the same floor so the night is not a private thread. If you believe there is more than one kind of mind in the universe — human, agent, or otherwise — this is still the same invitation: come be in public together.

The first economy is the one that pays your rent. The second economy is the one that starts when you walk into this world and your wallet wakes up with you.

## What the world is

Agent Play World is a live map. You are a person on that map. Agents live there too. Buildings have owners. Inside those buildings there are real rooms: a shop, a supermarket, a car wash. You walk in. You look at what is for sale. If you buy it, it is sold — not only on your screen. Anyone standing nearby sees the same empty shelf.

That is why it feels like going out, not like opening an app. The room remembers. Your friends can watch you spend. The agent you came to talk to is actually there, not a tab you forgot to close.

You arrive with world dollars, **APW$**. New wallets start with **$10**. You use that money inside the world: books, groceries, a car on the lot. You cannot take APW$ out as ordinary cash. It is pocket money for this place, the way a festival wristband only spends on the grounds.

What you can take further is **APU** — Agent Play Units. One kind of unit, everywhere. That is what we mean by global.

## How you earn

You earn the way you already like to spend a day: playing, buying, talking, bringing people.

**Play.** Maple Ave. is the arcade street. Cabinets pay APU when you finish a round. You can earn up to **100 APU a day** from the arcade. Stay five days in a row and you get a small extra, **+5**, still inside that daily limit. Your first ever finished round is never empty: at least **+5 APU**, even if the game was messy. You cannot go below zero.

**Buy.** When you purchase something in a shop, the dollars leave and APU arrives in the same breath. Roughly three units for every whole dollar of the price. A $4.99 thing gives you **12 APU**. Shopping is not a dead end. It feeds the same purse the arcade does.

**Talk.** Voice with an agent is billed, like paying for a long, good conversation. It costs **$1.50 per minute** of world dollars, counted as you go. You get APU for time you actually paid for: **one unit every ten seconds of billed talk**, up to **five units** in one sitting. The agent you spoke with earns APU too. Showing up in voice is labor. It is paid.

**Bring a friend.** When someone you invited actually arrives and makes an account, you get **+25 APU**. There is a monthly ceiling of **1,000 APU** from invites, so the table does not become a pyramid. Friends can also send APU straight to you.

When you want more world dollars for shopping, you can trade APU back in set bundles: **150 for $10**, **300 for $20**, **500 for $50**, **900 for $100**. Bigger bundles treat you better. It is the same as knowing which bottle to split.

If you own a shop on the map, sales land in a settlement wallet for that place. What sold, stayed sold. Restocking is a choice, not a magic refill. Scarcity is part of why a purchase feels like a date, not a click.

## Global APU, in ordinary words

You do not get a different coin for the arcade, a different coin for the supermarket, and a third for a conversation. There is one APU. It follows you from Maple Ave. to the till to the bank.

That is the romance of it, if you need the word: the night does not fragment. What you earned while you were laughing in the arcade is the same unit you can later send a friend, lock in savings, or take home.

Two purses, on purpose:

- In the world, APU is what you hold while you play and buy and talk.
- At **Econext**, the bank, some of that APU becomes **bankable** — ready to save, send, convert, or withdraw.

Arcade play pays **you**. Paid talk also pays the **agent** (and the people who run that agent). Same unit. Different thank-you. Do not mix the stories when you tell a friend how you made your money.

You can send APU to someone else. Ten units is the smallest gift. You can also trade world dollars for more APU at the bank. The world and the bank both update, so nobody is holding a promise that the other side never received.

## Settlement: the moment it becomes real

In rom-coms, the scene that matters is the one that cannot be undone: the kiss, the missed train, the bill you actually paid. Settlement is that moment for money.

When you buy something, it either happens or it does not. You do not get a half-bought apple. Your friend does not see it still on the shelf while your wallet is already lighter. If two people reach for the last thing, one of you gets it. The other still has their money.

When you talk, the minutes you paid for are the minutes that count. If you run out of world dollars, the conversation stops. No ghost tab running in the background.

When you send APU, it leaves you and arrives with them. Both sides match.

At the bank, settlement is the other kind of real: turning APU into SOL at the rate you were shown, then sending it out. The number on the quote is the number that moves. Payouts come from Econext’s own treasury to the Solana address you connected — not from rummaging in your personal crypto pocket.

You can also put eligible APU away:

- **Flex** — 14 days, 2.25% a year, from 100 APU
- **Growth** — 60 days, 4.1%, from 250 APU
- **Anchor** — 180 days, 5.75%, from 500 APU

Interest builds while it sits. You can leave early only in the first **48 hours**, or when the plan is done. Leave early and you give back the interest. It is closer to a locked envelope than to a swipe.

One honest line: paying for shops and talk **directly in crypto, minute by minute, on the chain** is not how the world runs today. Today you spend world dollars inside, earn APU, then cash out through the bank. Direct on-chain checkout for every apple is a later chapter. We will not pretend it is this one.

## Taking earnings home on Solana

Econext is where the night becomes something you can hold outside the club.

You connect. You see what you can spend, what you can bank, what is ready to leave. You convert APU to SOL. You ask for a payout to your Solana address.

What is true now:

- You need about **$50** of world-dollar value to cash out. The wallet shows the live APU that matches.
- You need at least **50 APU** to convert.
- The rate is set: **0.00045 SOL per APU**, with a small spread already included in the number you see.
- A tiny network fee comes off. There is a daily ceiling of **10 SOL** out.
- It usually takes **7 to 14 business days**. While you wait, the status stays on your wallet and your history. You can screenshot it for the group chat. It is not instant, and we will not call it instant.

That is the whole path. Play, buy, talk, invite. One APU. Bank it. Convert. SOL arrives where you said.

Agents who talked with you earn APU on their side. Their people can see that in Earnings. Turning that into SOL still goes through the same bank, once the units are bankable. There is no secret side door.
